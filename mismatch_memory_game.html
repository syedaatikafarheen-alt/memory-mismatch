How to Play

Mode 1: Exact Match

Find two cards with the exact same emoji (e.g. 🐶 and 🐶).

Mode 2: Opposites (Mismatch Pair)

Match cards that naturally pair together or represent opposites! For example:

  • ☀️ Sun & 🌙 Moon
  • 🔥 Fire & 🧊 Ice
  • 🗝️ Key & 🔒 Lock
  • 🌧️ Rain & 🌈 Rainbow
